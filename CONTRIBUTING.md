# Contributing

This is a published research prototype, not a collaborative project.

- **Pull requests are not accepted** and will be closed without review. This keeps the history and licensing simple; it is not a judgement of your change.
- **Forks for noncommercial use are welcome.** The code is under the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0 and the map data under the ODbL (see [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md) and [GPSLess/OfflineData/DATA-LICENSE.md](GPSLess/OfflineData/DATA-LICENSE.md)). Keep the `Required Notice` line and the licence files when you share a fork. Commercial use needs a separate licence from the author.
- **Issues are welcome** for bugs, questions and field results with other cars, phones and mounts. Use the issue template.

## Before you open an issue

Drive recordings are personal data. Even without GPS tracing they contain the start point and route you selected, estimated positions and timestamps; with GPS tracing they contain your real track. **Do not attach recordings, exported drives, device containers or screenshots that show where you live, work or park.** Instead include:

- app version and engine version (Sensor details, or the `engineVersion` recording header field),
- iPhone model, iOS version and how the phone is mounted,
- car wheelbase and whether automatic refinement was on,
- the road situation (straight, curvy, traffic jam, stop-and-go, roundabout) and what the app showed versus what happened,
- numbers from Sensor details (vibration speed, axle echo strength, turn and speed fixes) if relevant.

Security issues: open an issue without technical details and ask for a private contact.
