# User guide

The [Quick start](../README.md#quick-start) covers installation. This guide explains each control and the optional GPS reference trace.

## First drive

1. Park on a road represented in the map. Choose the map under **More → Map region** (Kyiv or Lviv region; only while no drive is active), then tap your current road. Taps farther than 70 metres from a selectable road are rejected.
2. Hold the green marker briefly, then drag to adjust it along that road. Connected roads become available at junctions. Tap a different road to explicitly relocate the starting point.
3. Use **Flip direction** so the arrow points forward along the car. On one-way roads only the mapped driving direction is offered.
   After selecting A, use **Flip direction** if needed and press **Confirm**. Tap point B. The app calculates up to three legal offline routes: the fastest by road-class speed, the shortest, and distinct alternatives no more than 50% slower that share under 75% of their length with routes already offered. Choose one in the list (others are drawn grey), then **Start**. You must follow the blue route: there is no automatic rerouting. Pausing retains the route; while paused and resumable, you can pick another offered route or **Change route from here** to plan new options from the paused estimate to the same point B.
   The magnifier searches the loaded region offline for villages, towns, districts and streets, in Ukrainian or Latin letters with one-typo tolerance (Slavske, Тухолька, "шевченка славсько"). Over the satellite view, which is Apple's map, it lists Apple Maps suggestions (addresses and businesses; online, only the typed text is sent) above the offline results. While choosing A or B, a result places the point on the nearest road within 400 m; otherwise it only moves the map. Reaching the estimated route end stops tracking and requests a new starting point; this is not an independently verified arrival.
4. Secure the phone in portrait with the top edge up and screen facing straight back along the car, not angled sideways toward the driver. A backward tilt is allowed; laying the phone flat is not supported. Use a rigid mount: speed is measured from road vibration reaching the phone.
   Set **Car wheelbase** (front-to-rear axle distance from your car's specifications) in the mount sheet or **Sensor details → Vehicle**. Speed and distance scale with it; the default is 2.70 m. With **Refine from route turns** on (the default), the app then measures it while you drive a locked route.
5. Press **Start tracking**. Optionally enable **Use GPS tracing** in this sheet to save reference data, granting When In Use location permission and Precise Location for useful measurements. Confirm the car is stopped and the mount is aligned, and remain still for four seconds. Then drive forward. The screen stays awake while tracking, and you can switch to another app, pull down Notification Center or lock the screen: with Location allowed (asked once when the mount sheet opens) the drive keeps running in the background under the blue location indicator. That coarse location session only keeps the app awake; its fixes are discarded. Without Location permission, leaving the app pauses tracking. The tracking card shows **Turn fixes** (turns matched to the route) and **Speed fixes** (road-bump speed measurements that corrected the inertial speed by 5 km/h or more, each announced briefly). GPS tracing is also configurable under **Sensor details → Field testing** while paused or selecting a position.
6. Press **I need the phone** before handling it. If the car moved while paused, stop and **Set current position**. Resume at the previous estimate is offered only when its road match remains sufficiently unambiguous; it retains the previous uncertainty.
7. If stopped speed is visibly wrong, remain stopped and untouched for two seconds, then use **Car is stopped · reset speed**. This supplies an explicit zero-speed observation and, when the preceding readings are settled, recalibrates gravity and all three gyro offsets. Old engine residual biases are cleared when this input reference changes. This does not correct position. Record the displayed speed before pressing it when assessing a field test.
8. After the drive, open **Recorded drives → Export all test data**. Send the files to your Mac with AirDrop or save them to a folder. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

Use a passenger for observations and controls during test drives. A successful build does not establish driving accuracy. This is an experimental tracker, and the displayed speed and uncertainty radius are model estimates, not verified accuracy claims.

## Optional GPS reference tracing

**Use GPS tracing** is off on a new install and its preference persists. It collects only during an active calibration/tracking segment, including while the drive continues in the background. Pause, reset, interruption and replay stop collection. A new collection uses a new location-manager instance; stale callbacks from previous instances are discarded, and the recording queue checks the original drive UUID before writing any reference row. Denied permission or unavailable GPS does not block inertial tracking. The live trace status distinguishes permission failure, waiting for a fix and reported low accuracy.

The most recent fix in each Core Location callback is recorded at most once per second, using receipt uptime to bound storage even if the wall clock changes. `gps-reference` rows share the drive's existing compressed file, sequence numbers and export controls. They retain fractional Unix acquisition/receipt timestamps, callback uptime, inferred acquisition uptime, fix age, coordinate, altitude, position/speed/course accuracy, reduced-accuracy authorization and software/accessory source flags. Negative unavailable values remain negative; non-finite values become absent and receive quality flags. Stale, cached, out-of-order, future, imprecise and simulated observations are retained with labels rather than silently converted into truth. Collection/permission/error events and observed delivery gaps are separate `gps-trace-event` rows. The endpoint events also retain the last fix times so a missing tail is visible.

GPS is **reference data only**: neither live motion processing, road matching, speed/stop calculations nor replay consumes these records. Tracing does not calibrate the engine automatically. Core Location is an iOS location solution, not a raw satellite measurement, and reported accuracy does not prove immunity to interference or spoofing. Later analysis must screen the reference and use separate drives for calibration and validation. Inferred acquisition uptime uses the receipt clock pair; `wall_clock_changed` flags identify a discontinuity requiring care during alignment.
